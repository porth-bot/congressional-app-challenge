Findings

Phone calls carry less soundPhone lines keep about 300 to 3,400 Hz. We ran 6,000 real and fake clips through three real codecs to measure the damage.
Breath and pause cues don’t surviveWeak even in the studio, gone on the phone.
A learned speech model does far betterOn studio audio, WavLM features cut the error on new attacks from 26% to 9%.
Phone audio needs its own cut-off and trainingReal voices flagged on an unseen codec: 63% with a studio cut-off, 24% with a phone cut-off, 14% with phone training too. Missed fakes rose from 4% to 20%.

Components

VoiceWavLM speech features (layer 4) and logistic regression, trained on landline and VoIP copies. It needs call audio, so test calls use its typical score for that voice.
LanguageWord and word-pair weights with logistic regression, trained on AI-generated scam dialogues and 160 AI-written family calls. Runs live in this browser, and catches human scammers too.
Safe wordSaying it multiplies the odds by 0.011, missing it by 9.9. Assumes family know it 9 times in 10 and scammers 1 in 100.
FusionBayes’ rule from an assumed 1 in 10 starting chance. A real-sounding voice can at most halve the odds, since human scammers sound real too.
StackPython, PyTorch, Hugging Face, scikit-learn, librosa, Praat, ffmpeg. App: HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Live phone streaming isn’t built yet.

Study 1: Phone-audio benchmark

Done
Data6,000 clips, half real, half computer-made. Trained on ASVspoof 2019; tested on its eval set (11 of 13 attacks unseen) and In-the-Wild.
Phone simulationLandline G.711, VoIP Opus 8 kbps, and cellular-style G.723.1, which no model trains on.
Models125 hand-measured features with gradient boosting; WavLM features with logistic regression. Each trained on studio and on phone copies, plus a cut-off-only control.
MetricsError rate, false alarms and misses at a cut-off fixed on training data. Intervals resample speakers.

Most of the drop comes from the phone cut-off; phone training adds the rest and lowers the error rate from 20% to 17%, but missed fakes rise from 4% to 20%.
